Adobe CMap resources (predefined CJK CMaps)

Nexus PDF includes, through the hayro-cmap 0.1.0 crate (its assets/cmaps.brotli, compiled in with
the crate's embed-cmaps feature, which the vendored hayro and hayro-interpret enable by default),
the predefined CMaps for the Adobe-CNS1, Adobe-GB1, Adobe-Japan1 and Adobe-Korea1 character
collections from Adobe's cmap-resources (https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources), as
packed by https://github.com/LaurenzV/cmap-resources/tree/hayro. They are character-code mapping
data, used only to read CJK PDFs. They are redistributed under the following licence, reproduced
